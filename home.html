Why UmmahLink

Built around your first year, not just your first day

The months after shahadah can be the loneliest and the most meaningful of your life, often at once. Every part of UmmahLink answers one question: what do you need right now to feel supported?

Belong

Learn

Practice

Gather

Questions

Ask anonymously, always
QIs it normal to still feel unsure sometimes, even after shahadah?
Completely. Faith grows in waves for almost everyone here — converts and lifelong Muslims alike. Bring it to a circle or ask another question any time.
QWhat do I say if my family doesn't understand my decision?
You're not alone in this — many members have been through it. Several converts in the community are happy to talk through what helped them.
