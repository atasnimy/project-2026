About UmmahLink

Our Mission

UmmahLink exists so no one has to go through their first year as a Muslim alone. We connect new Muslims to community, events, and the foundations of faith — all in a judgment-free, welcoming space.

Why We Started

Too many converts experience isolation after shahadah. The excitement of embracing Islam can quickly turn into loneliness when you don't know where to go, who to ask, or how to learn the basics. We built UmmahLink to be the bridge — connecting you with people who understand exactly what you're going through.
